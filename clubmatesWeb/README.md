# Clubmates

## Overview

Clubmates is a nightlife social discovery platform.

The idea is to help people who want to go clubbing or experience nightlife but don't currently have someone to go with.

Users will eventually be able to discover people with similar nightlife plans, connect with them, form plans and go out together.

Clubmates should NOT be positioned purely as a dating app.

The product is about:

- Social discovery
- Finding clubmates
- Nightlife
- Making real-world plans
- Finding a +1 or group
- Club discovery
- Reducing the friction of going out alone

## Visual Identity

Clubmates uses a modern nightlife-inspired visual identity.

Primary colours:

- Near Black: `#0A0B12`
- Deep Purple: `#1A0B2E`
- Violet: `#8A2BE2`
- Magenta: `#D946EF`
- Pink: `#FF2D95`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

The Clubmates symbol combines the idea of:

- C + M
- Two people
- Connection

The design should feel:

- Young
- Premium
- Social
- Modern
- Safe
- Energetic
- Simple

Avoid making the product look like a generic dating app.

## Product Principle

Clubmates should make the concept understandable within a few seconds:

> Want to go out but don't have someone to go with?

> Find your Clubmate.

Keep the experience simple.

Do not overload the website with features that do not yet exist.
